Infiltration Loot Guide

Max-rolled stats for generated items on ClassicPvP — Infiltration ruleset. Values shown are the absolute maximum possible for Tier 6 loot. Most drops will be lower; this guide shows what a perfectly-rolled item looks like.

Armor — Maximum Armor Level (AL)

Armor Level is added to your base weenie AL at item creation. All regular armor uses the same mutation table — T6 adds +100 to +165 AL (the top 5% of rolls land at +165 including ±5 random variance). Only one mutation fires per item. Covenant armor works differently — it sets AL absolutely rather than adding to a base.

Melee Weapons — Maximum Damage

Damage is added to the base weenie value at item creation. The table shows max T6 damage: the absolute top roll requiring wield 400 skill. Common T6 drops will be somewhat lower. Damage Variance (lower = better): controls how consistently you deal damage. A variance of 0.40 means your minimum hit is 60% of max damage. A variance of 0.25 means your minimum is 75% of max — much more consistent. Variance is always SET by mutation (not added), regardless of base weenie variance.

Weapon Speed Modifier

All T6 weapons are guaranteed to get a speed modifier (100% chance). Lower is faster. The quality roll at T6 goes up to level 9, with the formula:
speedMod = 1.0 − (qualityLevel × 0.025 ± 0.025 random)

At quality 9 (max T6 quality): speed mod = 0.75 to 0.80 (20–25% faster than unmodded base).

Missile Defense & Magic Defense (10% chance)

All melee weapons have a 10% chance to also roll a Missile Defense Modifier and/or Magic Defense Modifier, both in the range 1.005–1.025. These are small bonuses but apply at all tiers equally.

Wands & Casters

Wands can roll up to three types of bonuses: Elemental Damage Mod (only on elemental wands), Mana Conversion Mod, and Melee Defense Mod. All three roll independently.
