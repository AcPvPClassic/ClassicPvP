## 🎯 Weapon Combat Modifiers

> Every weapon can roll additional combat modifiers on top of damage. These apply to all melee weapon types (swords, axes, maces, spears, daggers, staves, unarmed) with identical max values.

### Attack Modifier (WeaponOffense)

| Tier | Range |
|------|-------|
| T1 | +0 to +0.05 |
| T2 | +0.02 to +0.07 |
| T3 | +0.03 to +0.10 |
| T4 | +0.05 to +0.12 |
| T5 | +0.06 to +0.15 |
| **T6 Max** | **+0.15 → final 1.15** |

Base is 1.0. A 1.15 attack mod means 15% better chance to land hits.

### Melee Defense Modifier (WeaponDefense)

| Tier | Range |
|------|-------|
| T1 | +0 to +0.05 |
| T2 | +0.02 to +0.07 |
| T3 | +0.03 to +0.10 |
| T4 | +0.05 to +0.12 |
| T5 | +0.06 to +0.15 |
| **T6 Max** | **+0.15 → final 1.15** |

Same range as attack mod. Stacks with your base melee defense skill.

### Weapon Speed Modifier

> **⭐ All T6 weapons are guaranteed to get a speed modifier (100% chance).** Lower is faster. The quality roll at T6 goes up to level 9, with the formula:
>
> ```
> speedMod = 1.0 − (qualityLevel × 0.025 ± 0.025 random)
> ```
>
> At quality 9 (max T6 quality): speed mod = **0.75 to 0.80** (20–25% faster than unmodded base).

| Tier | Chance of Getting Speed Bonus | Max Speed Mod at That Tier |
|------|-------------------------------|----------------------------|
| T1 | 50% | ~0.98 (quality 1) |
| T2 | 60% | ~0.95 (quality 2) |
| T3 | 70% | ~0.93 (quality 4) |
| T4 | 80% | ~0.88 (quality 6) |
| T5 | 90% | ~0.83 (quality 7) |
| **T6** | **100%** | **~0.75 (quality 9 — best)** |

### Missile Defense & Magic Defense (10% chance)

> All melee weapons have a **10% chance** to also roll a Missile Defense Modifier and/or Magic Defense Modifier, both in the range **1.005–1.025**. These are small bonuses but apply at all tiers equally.

## 🪄 Wands & Casters

> Wands can roll up to three types of bonuses: Elemental Damage Mod (only on elemental wands), Mana Conversion Mod, and Melee Defense Mod. All three roll independently.

### Elemental Damage Modifier — *elemental wands only*

> ⚠️ The elemental damage mod MULTIPLIES your spell damage for the matching element. A 1.10 mod means 10% more damage. Higher mods require higher wield requirements.

| Tier | Mod Range | Wield Required |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| T1–T4 | 1.01–1.02 | None |
| T5 | 1.01–1.02 | None |
| T5 | 1.03–1.05 | 310 skill |
| T6 | 1.01–1.05 | None / 310 (for 1.03+) |
| T6 | 1.06–1.08 | 330 skill |
| **T6 Best** | **1.09–1.10** | 355 skill |

*1.10 is the absolute max, achievable only with a 355 skill wield requirement. These are extremely rare.*

## 🏹 Missile Weapons — Bows & Crossbows

> Missile weapons roll a **Damage Modifier** (multiplier to arrow/bolt damage) and, for elemental variants, an **Elemental Damage Bonus** (flat bonus to the element). Non-elemental and elemental bows use different mutation tables.

### Elemental Bows

| Stat | T1–T5 | T6 | Notes |
|------|-------|----|-------|
| Damage Modifier | 2.23–2.30 | **2.23–2.30** | Consistent across all tiers — locked in range |
| Elemental Damage Bonus | +1 to +4 | **+1 to +12** | Higher bonuses need higher wield (see below) |

**T6 Elemental Damage Bonus Breakdown**

| Bonus Range | Wield Required | Relative Chance |
|-------------|----------------|-----------------|
| +1 to +4 | None | Common (50%) |
| +5 to +8 | 335 skill | Uncommon (35%) |
| **+9 to +12** | 360 skill | Rare (15%) |

### Non-Elemental Bows

| Tier | Damage Modifier | Wield Required |
|------|-----------------|----------------|
| T1 | 1.50–2.00 | None |
| T2 | up to 2.10 | 250 (for 2.03+) |
| T3 | 2.03–2.20 | 250–270 |
| T4 | 2.13–2.30 | 270–290 |
| T5–T6 | **2.23–2.30** | 290 skill |

*At T5 and T6, non-elemental bows match elemental bows for Damage Modifier (2.23–2.30). The tradeoff is no Elemental Damage Bonus.*

> **⭐ Best-in-slot missile weapon:** A T6 elemental bow with Damage Modifier 2.30 and Elemental Damage Bonus +12 (wield 360). The +12 elemental bonus is the rarest roll — expect to see most T6 elemental bows with +1 to +8.
